6 · Do & Don't

✔ Keep the gradient direction top → bottom, always.
✔ Use the mono version over photography or busy surfaces.
✔ The mark is one SVG — crisp at every size, from favicon to billboard.
✘ Never recolor the G or add an outline to the mark.
✘ Never rotate, stretch, or skew the shield.
✘ Never use Core Orange text smaller than 12px on dark surfaces.
