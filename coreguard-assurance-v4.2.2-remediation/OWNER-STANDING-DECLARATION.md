## 1. The standing exception, in the owner's own voice

> **Single-operator exception accepted by the project owner. Independent review is
> unavailable in this project's current composition. Transaction authorization remains
> the sole responsibility of the named human operator.**

This declaration makes the exception **standing and explicit** rather than implied: the
C7/C8 approvals in [`GOVERNANCE-RECORDS.md`](./GOVERNANCE-RECORDS.md) are documented risk
acceptances by the author/owner pair — **not independent reviews** — and the owner accepts
them *as exactly that*, no more and no less. The disclosure headers on those records are
the product's honesty, not its weakness; removing one voids the record.

## 3. The P7 boundary (binding on every future record and tool)

1. **No private key, seed, or secret material** may ever be entered into, produced by,
   stored in, or transmitted through this repository, its tooling, or any chat session.
2. **No authorization may be inferred from technical test results** — 713/713, 46/46,
   CI-green, or any successor number is evidence, never permission.
3. **No broadcast without the named human operator's explicit approval** on a completed
   [`EXECUTION-AUTHORIZATION.md`](./EXECUTION-AUTHORIZATION.md) (P7) — signed in person,
   with the transaction data reviewed **outside** any session.
4. The agent's role ends at preparing, verifying, and accurately describing the papers;
   it structurally cannot be the operator and cannot grant a financial/network
   authorization on anyone's behalf.

## 4. Effect of this declaration

- C7/C8/C9 stand as **executed single-operator approvals** — the honest maximum for this
  project's composition, with the automatic upgrade path (GOVERNANCE-MODE §4) unchanged:
  any independent human countersignature upgrades the chain, no re-work.
- The governance posture is fully disclosed to any future reader, reviewer, or counterparty.
- Nothing in this declaration widens C9's narrow scope or pre-fills any P7 field; the
  operator's §3/§5 blanks in the P7 template remain the only place execution can begin.
