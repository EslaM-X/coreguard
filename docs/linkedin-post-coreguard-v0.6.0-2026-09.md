Execution is not consent. Consent is not adjudication. Adjudication is not
settlement.

Today we released CoreGuard v0.6.0, including a reproducible 90-second
demonstration of its verification pipeline.

The demo runs repository-native verifiers for:

- execution evidence
- consent-state separation
- ADAL/1 dispute packaging
- SHA-256 tamper detection
- reference tribunal behavior
- structural interoperability boundaries

The important part is what CoreGuard does not claim:

- no adjudication by the evidence layer
- no automatic settlement
- no silent conversion of UNKNOWN into a fact
- no live People's Court integration claim
- no external endorsement claim

The People's Court adapter is explicitly structural and offline/dry-run.

The release is designed around a simple question:

Can an independent person take the repository, run the same verification path,
and inspect the result without trusting the project author?

That's the standard we're building toward.
