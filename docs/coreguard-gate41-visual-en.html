fail-closed semantics: a language that cannot be forged

A binding contract for engine 2.1.2 — developed after a negative scenario was found to "silently lose its precondition" while success was still being announced.

StatusMeaningCounts as success in a mandatory negative scenario?
PASSThe condition genuinely held, with evidencein expect-pass cases only
FAILA violation was caught by a closed checkYes — that is the goal in expect-fail
BLOCKEDCould not proceed, blocker documentedYes — provided the blocker is documented
INCONCLUSIVEInsufficient evidence to judgeNo
NOT_APPLICABLENot applicable, with a documented reasonNo — ever (the old Scenario 9 defect)
PRECONDITION_FAILUREA mandatory precondition was absentFails the whole cycle: exit 1
Golden rules shipped: NOT_APPLICABLE ≠ PASS · a missing manifest/evidence file = an explicit refusal, never a bypass · any unexplained difference halts the cycle and is never fixed by editing the report · the declared scenario count = the actual count (cosmetic Scenario 15 was removed: 17 → 16; the real failure it gestured at is covered by 14).
