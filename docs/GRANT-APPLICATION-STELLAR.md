# Stellar Community Fund Application

## Project: CoreGuard Global — Cross-Border Payment Verification

### 1. Project Overview
CoreGuard extends its verifiable execution truth layer to Stellar, enabling cross-border payment verification with independently verifiable evidence.

### 2. Problem Statement
- Cross-border payments lack execution verification
- Payment failures are hard to diagnose
- No independently verifiable evidence of payment conformance
- Compliance teams trust, not verify

### 3. Solution
CoreGuard on Stellar:
- Soroban commitment anchor (deployed on testnet)
- Payment verification before submission
- Deterministic replay after submission
- Fail-closed: UNKNOWN → HELD
